# The Silent Co-Driver

## Idea
The Silent Co-Driver is a motorsport intelligence dashboard that listens to the driver’s radio communications and turns that voice data into live performance insight. Instead of relying only on telemetry numbers, it interprets tone, stress, and emotional cues from the driver’s audio to detect pressure moments during a race.

## Problem it solves
In racing, small emotional spikes often appear before a driver loses pace or makes a mistake. Traditional telemetry dashboards show laps, speed, and tire data, but they rarely capture what the driver is experiencing in the moment. This means signaled stress, panic, fatigue, or confidence shifts are missed when they matter most.

## Core concept
The product combines:
- speech recognition to transcribe radio calls,
- acoustic and emotional analysis to estimate stress, calmness, or fatigue,
- lap-time correlation to connect those emotional signals to race performance.

This creates a “co-driver” layer that helps teams understand not just what happened on track, but how the driver was feeling when it happened.

## Why it matters
Race strategy and performance decisions become much more effective when teams can see how the driver is responding under pressure. The system helps identify moments where the driver is under stress, where pace drops, and which laps were emotionally affected.

## Use case
A pit wall or race engineer can use the dashboard to:
- listen to radio clips from the session,
- see mood classification and confidence scores,
- inspect the transcript,
- compare emotional state to lap-time performance,
- detect patterns that may explain a slow lap or a risky decision.

## Product vision
This tool is designed like an AI-powered race intelligence cockpit for motorsport teams, turning voice and telemetry data into a clear, actionable story of race pressure and performance.
